# La Vuelta del Coyote

### Mountain Biking · Adventure · Los Santos, Costa Rica 🇨🇷

**La Vuelta del Coyote** is a website dedicated to showcasing a mountain biking competition in the Los Santos region of Costa Rica. Its goal is to provide a digital experience where visitors can learn about the race courses, explore available packages, discover the prizes, and access the information they need to participate in the event.

**Website:** [lavueltadelcoyote.vercel.app](https://lavueltadelcoyote.vercel.app/home.html)

---

## Key Features

- **Home Page:** Introduction to the event and its adventure-focused concept.
- **Event Information:** Details about the competition and its organization.
- **Schedule:** Overview of scheduled activities and events.
- **Mountain Biking Courses:** Routes categorized by difficulty level.
- **Competition Packages:** Presentation of available options, prices, and benefits.
- **Registration:** Access to information about participating in the competition.
- **Prizes:** Display of prizes associated with each course.
- **Gallery:** Visual content related to the event.
- **Contact:** Contact form for inquiries and communication with visitors.
- **Responsive Design:** An interface designed to provide a smooth browsing experience across different devices.

## Technologies

- **HTML5:** Structure and organization of website content.
- **CSS3:** Styling, visual design, and component presentation.
- **JavaScript:** Interactivity and dynamic functionality.
- **Vercel:** Website hosting and deployment.

## Project Objectives

- Create an attractive digital presence for a sporting event.
- Organize information about courses, packages, and prizes.
- Provide easy access to competition details.
- Improve the visitor browsing experience.
- Apply web design and development skills in a practical project.
